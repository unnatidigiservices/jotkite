**You do *not* need one** to run unmodified JotKite on your own website or your company's website, including a commercial business website. The AGPL allows that.

**You *do* need one** if you want any of the following without meeting the AGPL's conditions:

| You want to… | AGPL requires… | Commercial license lets you… |
|---|---|---|
| Modify JotKite and run it for clients or users over a network | Publish your modified source to those users | Keep your changes private |

| White-label it (remove JotKite branding or attribution) | Keep legal notices intact | Rebrand it as your own |
| Bundle it inside a proprietary product or theme you sell | License the combined work under AGPL | Ship it under your own terms |
